---
title: Data and privacy
description: Where ROXY stores your data and what it sends over the network.
---

| OS | Folder |
|---|---|
| Linux | `~/.config/roxy/` |
| Windows | `%APPDATA%\roxy\` |

Files:

- `config.json` — providers, keys, accent, behaviour.
- `sessions.json` — every transcript.
- `roxy.log` — one line per completed turn (provider, model, duration, size).

## Privacy

No telemetry, no analytics, no ROXY account. Delete the folder for a clean
slate. Keys are stored in plain text on your disk; protect the folder like any
other credential store. The only network traffic is to the providers you
configured.
