---
title: Sessions
description: Create, organise, switch models, and stop generation.
---

- **Create** — **New chat** in the rail, or `Ctrl/⌘ + N`. Titles derive from
  your first message unless you turn that off.
- **Organise** — search the session list, rename inline, pin, archive or
  delete. Deletion asks first by default.
- **Switch model** — the model button above the composer opens a searchable
  picker grouped by provider. There is no “Auto” entry: you always see what
  will answer.
- **Stop** — `Esc` or the stop button cancels the request; for CLIs it
  terminates the child process.
