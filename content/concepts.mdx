Three words cover most of the app.

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Session** | One conversation. It stores every message, the provider and model that answered each turn, and token counts. |
| **Provider** | A source of models: an OpenAI-compatible API, a local agent CLI, or the 9Router endpoint. Providers are what the picker lists. |
| **Route** | The provider + model currently answering. Changing the route never edits the transcript. |

## Switching models mid-thread

When you switch models, ROXY inserts a small handoff marker in the transcript
and replays the full user/assistant history to the new model. Nothing is
summarised or dropped. The marker is carried into the transcript but never sent
to a model.
